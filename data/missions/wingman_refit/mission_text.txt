Location: the host's fleet
Date: now

Refit your ship in a friend's fleet with the game's own refit screen.

In CO-OP, pick your ship, open its refit page (R) and press G. Then come here, click the ship and choose Refit. Change what you like: it is saved as you go. Then go back to CO-OP. Your changes are loaded into the ship's refit page, where you see what the host has and what it costs, and send them to the host.

You don't need to start this mission. If you do, it ends at once.
